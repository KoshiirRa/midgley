#01

When Does Context Routing Help? A Systematic Study of Multi-Modal Fusion in Time Series Forecasting

Aug 2026 arXiv:2608.25128v1 PDF

Authors

Ruizhe Zhou, Gaoyuan Du, Xiaoyang Liu, et al.

Target Modules

src/feature_engineering.py

Methodology Type

Pre-Training Diagnostic & RBU Bound

Key Theorem & Mathematical Formulation

$$\mathrm{MMSE}(X_{t+h} \mid X_t, C) = \sigma^2 (1 - \rho_h^2) \cdot 2^{-2\delta}, \quad \mathrm{RBU} = 1 - 2^{-2\delta}$$

Calculates rolling autocorrelation $\rho_h = \mathrm{Corr}(X_t, X_{t+h})$. When $\rho_h > 0.95$ (calm baseline markets), last-value shortcuts dominate and LLM qualitative context fusion is suppressed to prevent hallucination. When $\rho_h \le 0.95$ (exogenous shock events), event memory decay and shock multipliers are dynamically enabled.

#02

CEDAR: Controlled and Event-Driven Demand Forecasting via Residual Decomposition

Aug 2026 arXiv:2608.25871v1 PDF

Authors & Institution

Junjie Meng, Ranxu Zhang, Zi-an Zhang, Chao Wang et al. (Alibaba 1688)

Target Modules

src/event_analyzer.py, src/models.py

Methodology Type

Two-Stage Decoupled Residual Decomposition

Key Theorem & Mathematical Formulation

$$\mathbf{s}_{t+1} = f_\theta(\mathbf{s}_{\le t}, \mathbf{a}_{\le t+1}) + \epsilon_t, \quad \hat{y}_{\mathrm{final}} = f_{\mathrm{quant}}(X) + \hat{\epsilon}_{\mathrm{event}}$$

Decouples baseline quantitative time-series transitions from qualitative event shock residuals ($\epsilon_t$). Stage I fits regularized Ridge regression on numerical commodity fundamentals; Stage II prompts Gemini 2.5 Flash for Tag Filtering & Regional Calendar Event Synthesis, outputting calibrated residual delta adjustments $\hat{\epsilon}_{\mathrm{event}}$.

#07

Advances in Financial Machine Learning (Purged CPCV & PBO Overfitting Audit)

Wiley 2018 Wiley Text

Author

Marcos López de Prado

Target Modules

src/models.py, src/feature_auditor.py

Methodology Type

Purged & Combinatorial Purged Cross-Validation (CPCV)

Key Theorem & Mathematical Formulation

$$\text{Purge Condition: } i \in \text{Train} \iff [t_{i,\text{start}}, t_{i,\text{end}}] \cap [t_{j,\text{test,start}}, t_{j,\text{test,end}} + h_{\text{embargo}}] = \emptyset \quad \forall j \in \text{Test}, \quad \text{PBO} = \int_{-\infty}^0 f(\lambda) \, d\lambda$$

Purges overlapping forward label windows with post-test embargoes ($h_{\text{embargo}} = 5\text{ days}$) to eliminate temporal lookahead data leakage in 5-day horizon forecasting. Implements CSCV Probability of Backtest Overfitting (PBO) and Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR) in src/feature_auditor.py (Issue #146).

#10

Dynamic Volatility-Gated Persistence Blending (DV-GPB) & Empirical Residual Calibration

Sep 2026 Midgley Proprietary

Authors

Midgley Quantitative Research Group

Target Modules

src/models.py, src/dynamic_region.py

Methodology Type

Adaptive Sigmoid Gating & Empirical 95% Residual CI

Key Theorem & Mathematical Formulation

$$\lambda_{\text{vol}} = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-200.0 \cdot (\sigma_{14\text{d}} - 0.015)}}, \quad \hat{y}_{t+5} = \lambda_{\text{vol}} \hat{y}_{\text{model}} + (1 - \lambda_{\text{vol}}) y_t, \quad \text{CI}_{95\%} = \hat{y}_{t+5} \pm 1.96 \cdot \sigma_{\text{residual, 30d}}(r)$$

Transitions model predictions smoothly toward pure naive persistence during low-volatility plateaus ($\sigma_{14\text{d}} \ll 0.015$) while maintaining 100% responsiveness to real-world event shocks ($\sigma_{14\text{d}} > 0.015$), coupled with empirical 30-day residual standard error bounds ensuring $\ge 90\%$ coverage (Issue #214).
