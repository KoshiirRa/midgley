Chicagoland Multi-Tier Fuel Taxes & Cross-State Border Arbitrage

Models commuter fuel purchasing substitution between Cook County, IL (subject to state, county, municipal fuel taxes plus up to 10.25% composite sales tax) and Northwest Indiana (Lake and Porter counties) driven by a persistent $0.35-$0.55/gal cross-border retail price differential along the I-80/I-90/I-94 corridors.

Great Lakes Refining Complex & Midwest Pipeline Confluence

Chicagoland rack supplies are anchored by BP's 435,000 bpd Whiting IN refinery (6th largest in the US), CITGO's 177,000 bpd Lemont refinery along the Des Plaines River, and ExxonMobil's 251,000 bpd Joliet refinery. Inbound Western Canadian crude arrives via Enbridge pipelines, while outbound refined product disperses via the Wolverine, West Shore, and Badger pipelines.

Multi-Layered Municipal Tax Structure & Statutory Fees
