PADD 1C Infrastructure & Selma Hub Dynamics

Greenville, NC (Pitt County) sits within the PADD 1C Lower Atlantic petroleum distribution corridor. Gasoline supplies originate from Gulf Coast refiners via Colonial Pipeline (Line 1 Gasoline / Line 2 Distillates), breaking out at major junction tank farms in Selma, NC (55 miles west) and Apex, NC before tank-truck dispatch across Eastern NC.

Colonial Pipeline Breakout Hubs Selma & Apex NC tank farms act as primary wholesale rack pricing hubs for Pitt, Lenoir & Beaufort counties.
