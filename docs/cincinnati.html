Tri-State Petroleum Supply Chain & Tax Mechanics

Refining & River Logistics

Cincinnati marine terminals (Mile 470 on Ohio River) receive refined fuel via barges coming up the Lower Mississippi River through the Cairo, IL confluence from Gulf Coast refiners, supplemented by Marathon's 291,000 bpd Catlettsburg KY refinery and Buckeye Pipeline.

Dual-State Rack Margin Equations

$$\text{Rack Margin}_{\text{OH}} = P_{\text{OH Retail}} - P_{\text{Wholesale RBOB}} = \$3.450 - \$3.184 = \$0.266/\text{gal}$$

$$\text{Rack Margin}_{\text{KY}} = P_{\text{KY Retail}} - P_{\text{Wholesale RBOB}} = \$3.325 - \$3.184 = \$0.141/\text{gal}$$

Reflects the $0.125/gal state fuel tax differential (OH: $0.385 vs KY: $0.260).
