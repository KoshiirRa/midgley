Regional Econometric Drivers & Physical Infrastructure Factors

New York Harbor Pricing Epicenter, High Statutory Tax Burden & Upstate Logistics Wedge

New York Harbor serves as the primary physical benchmark for NYMEX RBOB gasoline futures and Atlantic coast waterborne trading. Retail pricing across New York State reflects a complex synthesis of coastal barge economics, Phillips 66 Bayway refinery output, a heavy statutory state tax burden ($0.472/gal baseline before county sales surcharges), EPA summer RFG specifications across NYC and Long Island, and regional midstream trucking premiums across Upstate New York and the Adirondacks.

Bayway Refining Anchor, Buckeye Pipeline Corridors & Waterborne Barging

Regional product supply is anchored by Phillips 66 Bayway Refinery in Linden, NJ (258 kbpd), the largest refinery on the East Coast. Refined products move throughout New York via the Buckeye Pipeline system (supplying NYC airports, Long Island, Albany, and Rochester), Colonial Pipeline Line 1 terminus, and coastal barge lightering through the Arthur Kill, Hudson River, and Long Island Sound.

New York State Statutory Fuel Excise, Petroleum Business Tax & Sales Tax Structure

New York State imposes an aggregate statutory baseline motor fuel tax of $0.472/gal, comprising the state motor fuel excise tax ($0.0805/gal), Petroleum Business Tax (PBT, $0.1915/gal), state sales tax equivalent ($0.160/gal statutory baseline capped), and spill tax ($0.003/gal). Local county and municipal sales taxes add an additional $0.08 to $0.16/gal depending on jurisdiction, bringing combined state and local taxes to over $0.55/gal. Combined with the Federal excise tax ($0.184/gal), the statutory baseline tax burden is $0.656/gal.
