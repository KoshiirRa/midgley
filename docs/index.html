Multi-Agent Execution Pipeline & Model Hierarchy

Stages 1 → 7
Stage 1 & 2 Data & NLP
Extraction & Memory Fusion

Ingests Finlight headlines, NOAA alerts, maritime chokepoints & social feeds into Gemini 2.5 Flash. Decays shocks with \(t_{1/2} = 4.0\text{--}5.0\) days.

Stage 3 Main Model
Main Quantitative Model

Fits regularized Ridge/XGBoost on NYMEX RBOB (RB=F) & WTI Crude (CL=F) technicals + LLM event vector matrix to output base commodity benchmark forecast.

Stage 4 Metro Calibration
Localized Metro Models

Ingests Main Wholesale Base Forecast and calibrates for Tulsa OK ($3.89/gal), Newark DE ($3.35/gal), and Cincinnati OH/KY ($3.45/gal) with local rack margins & refinery dynamics.

Stage 5-7 MLOps & Review
Scenario Simulator & MLOps

Simulates counterfactual shocks (refinery outages, canal detours, executive posts). Logs out-of-time predictions and backfills actual prices on Saturdays.

6 Core Feature Ingestion Pillars

Multi-modal data feeds ingested into Stage 1 & 2

1. Quantitative Commodity Futures

NYMEX RBOB Gasoline Futures (RB=F) and WTI Crude (CL=F) build 3-2-1 refining crack spreads and moving average returns.

2. Gemini 2.5 Flash News

Real-time headlines from Finlight.me REST API translated into bounded 5-dimensional geopolitical factor impact vectors.

3. NOAA Weather Alerts

National Gulf Coast hurricane tracks, PADD refining alerts, polar vortex freeze warnings, and localized severe weather events.

4. Maritime Chokepoints

Strait of Hormuz (21M bpd) blockade risk & Red Sea / Suez rerouting freight premiums (+12 to +14 days).

5. Executive Social Feed

Econometric modeling of executive energy posts generating 1.42× higher Sunday market open gap volatility.

6. Alternative Physical Feeds

Cboe OVX options tail-risk volatility & Baker Hughes drilling rig counts tracking 3-6 month supply pipelines.
