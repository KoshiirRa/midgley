01

Quantitative Time-Series & 3-2-1 Crack Spreads

A crack spread measures the profit margin refiners earn when "cracking" crude oil into finished petroleum products. Because crude oil is quoted in dollars per barrel (\(42\text{ gallons}\) per barrel) while wholesale gas is quoted in dollars per gallon, we convert crude prices into per-gallon equivalents.

Equation 1.1: Refining Crack Spread & Technical Returns

$$\text{CrackSpread}_t = P_{\text{RBOB}, t} - \frac{P_{\text{WTI}, t}}{42.0}, \quad r_t = \ln\left(\frac{P_t}{P_{t-1}}\right)$$

where \(P_{\text{RBOB}}\) is the NYMEX RBOB Futures price (\(RB=F\)) and \(P_{\text{WTI}}\) is West Texas Intermediate Crude (\(CL=F\)). Moving averages \(\text{MA}_K(t) = \frac{1}{K}\sum_{i=0}^{K-1} P_{t-i}\) are calculated across \(K \in \{7, 14, 30\}\) trading days.

Qualitative LLM Extraction (Google Gemini 2.5 Flash)

Unstructured news bulletins and press releases are processed by Google Gemini 2.5 Flash to convert qualitative events into a bounded numerical factor vector space:

Equation 2.1: LLM Bounded Impact Vector Space

$$\mathbf{V}_{\text{event}, t} = \begin{bmatrix} S_{\text{geopolitical}} \\ S_{\text{supply}} \\ S_{\text{opec}} \\ S_{\text{demand}} \\ S_{\text{pressure}} \end{bmatrix}_t \in [-1.0, +1.0]^5$$

Each component is bounded in the interval \([-1.0, +1.0]\), representing negative (bearish), zero (neutral), or positive (bullish) market pressure.

Global & Regional Maritime Chokepoints, Inland River Barging & Waterborne Terminals

Global maritime chokepoints, inland river tow barge draft constraints, and coastal waterborne terminal freight logistics dictate crude transit delays, spot barge shipping rates, and regional rack margins:

Equation 4.1: Unified Waterway Freight & Detour Penalty Model

$$\Delta P_{\text{freight}, r} = \underbrace{C_{\text{tanker}} \times \left( \frac{\Delta \text{Distance}}{v_{\text{knot}}} \right)}_{\text{Global Chokepoint Detour}} + \underbrace{\gamma_{\text{draft}} \cdot \text{Index}_{\text{barge}}}_{\text{Inland River Tow Constraints}} + \underbrace{\Delta \text{Margin}_{\text{waterborne}, r}}_{\text{Coastal Terminal / Canal Surcharge}}$$
$$\text{Index}_{\text{barge}} = \max\left(0, \min\left(1, \frac{\text{Gage}_{\text{threshold}} - \text{Gage}_t}{\text{Gage}_{\text{threshold}} - \text{Gage}_{\text{min}}}\right)\right)$$

• Strait of Hormuz: \(21.0\text{M bpd}\) (\(20\%\) of global petroleum) naval blockade & Iranian mine threats (\(+\$0.109/\text{gal}\) futures price shock).
• Suez Canal & Bab-el-Mandeb / Red Sea: Cape of Good Hope reroutings add \(+12\text{--}14\) days transit time, adding \(+\$4.50/\text{bbl}\) marine freight premium (\(+\$0.201/\text{gal}\) price shock).
• Venezuela Orinoco & Caribbean: OFAC General License 44 heavy crude sanctions and tanker export quotas (\(0.85\text{M bpd}\)).

2. Inland River Barging & Hydro Navigation Corridors (Mid-Continent & Midwest):
• Cincinnati Tri-State (Ohio & Lower Mississippi River Corridor): Confluence at Cairo, IL (Mile 981 on Lower Mississippi / Mile 0 on Ohio River) and Memphis, TN USGS gage (07032000). Autumn low-water droughts drop gage below \(5.0\text{ ft}\) (severe crisis at \(<0.0\text{ ft}\)), enforcing \(-40\%\) barge payload draft cuts, surging spot barge freight rates \(+300\%\), and expanding Cincinnati/Ohio Valley rack margins by \(+14.5\text{¢}/\text{gal}\) (\(+\$0.145/\text{gal}\)). • Tulsa Metro (MKARNS / Port of Catoosa Navigation): McClellan-Kerr Arkansas River Navigation System (Head of Navigation Mile 445 at Verdigris River) linking West Tulsa HF Sinclair refinery corridor to the Mississippi River; high-discharge flood stages or low-water lock halts throttle refined product barge departures.

3. Coastal Waterborne Terminals & Domestic Canal Bottlenecks:
• Newark Metro (Delaware Bay & C&D Canal): Big Stone Anchorage deepwater tanker lightering freezes and C&D Canal emergency shoaling closures force tank barges onto a \(300\text{ nm}\) open-ocean detour around the Delmarva Peninsula (+35% marine freight rate surge, expanding regional Delaware rack margins by \(+\$0.097/\text{gal}\), \(p = 0.00191\)).

• Oakland & SF Bay Area (Carquinez Strait & San Francisco Bay): Marine crude lightering in Central San Francisco Bay and Carquinez Strait barge berthing supplying Chevron Richmond (\(245,000\text{ bpd}\)) and Martinez refineries; Pacific bomb cyclones and coastal gale warnings throttle marine dockings and CaRFG waterborne transfers.
• Greenville NC (Port of Wilmington & Cape Fear River): Coastal petroleum barge offloading at Port of Wilmington oil terminal docks supplying Eastern North Carolina fuel racks via Cape Fear River navigation channel during storm surge closures.

09

Standardized Ridge Estimator & Live Pump Calibration

Rather than predicting non-stationary raw price levels, our model fits a regularized Ridge Regression (\(\alpha = 10.0\)) model to predict 5-day percentage price returns (\(\Delta\%\)), applied directly to today's live pump price (\(P_{\text{Live Base}}\)):

Equation 9.1: Regularized Ridge Objective Function & Calibration

$$\min_{\boldsymbol{\beta}} \sum_{i=1}^{N} \left( y_i - \mathbf{x}_i^T \boldsymbol{\beta} \right)^2 + \alpha \|\boldsymbol{\beta}\|_2^2, \quad \hat{P}_{\text{Metro Retail}, t+5} = P_{\text{Live Base}} \times (1 + \hat{y}_{t+5})$$

where \(\alpha = 10.0\) prevents overfitting across high-dimensional hybrid features, achieving a record low out-of-time error of \(\text{MAE} = \$0.1069/\text{gal}\).

Equation 9.2: Purged & Combinatorial Cross-Validation (CPCV) Overlap Elimination (Issue #117)

$$\text{Purge Condition: } i \in \text{Train} \iff [t_{i,\text{start}}, t_{i,\text{end}}] \cap [t_{j,\text{test,start}}, t_{j,\text{test,end}} + h_{\text{embargo}}] = \emptyset \quad \forall j \in \text{Test}$$

Temporal Data Leakage Prevention (López de Prado, 2018): In 5-day step-ahead horizon forecasting, standard cross-validation leaks lookahead information because observation \(t\)'s evaluation window \([t, t+5]\) overlaps with test samples. PurgedGroupTimeSeriesSplit and CombinatorialPurgedCV purge any training observation whose label period intersects test folds or falls within the post-test embargo window (\(h_{\text{embargo}} = 5\text{ days}\)), ensuring 100% out-of-sample purity (queryable via REST API at GET /api/v1/forecast/purged-cv).

Dynamic Volatility-Gated Persistence Blending (DV-GPB) & Empirical CI (Issue #214)

During low-volatility plateaus, autoregressive time-series models risk overreacting to micro-fluctuations. DV-GPB applies a continuous sigmoid gate derived from 14-day rolling price volatility (\(\sigma_{14\text{d}}\)) to dynamically blend model forecasts with naive persistence:

Equation 12.1: Adaptive Sigmoid Volatility Gate & Empirical Residual Bounds

$$\lambda_{\text{vol}} = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-200.0 \cdot (\sigma_{14\text{d}} - 0.015)}}, \quad \hat{y}_{t+5} = \lambda_{\text{vol}} \hat{y}_{\text{model}} + (1 - \lambda_{\text{vol}}) y_t, \quad \text{CI}_{95\%} = \hat{y}_{t+5} \pm 1.96 \cdot \sigma_{\text{residual, 30d}}(r)$$

When \(\sigma_{14\text{d}} \ll 0.015\), \(\lambda_{\text{vol}} \to 0.0\) (shrinking forecasts to pure naive persistence). When \(\sigma_{14\text{d}} > 0.015\), \(\lambda_{\text{vol}} \to 1.0\) (preserving 100% of event shock vectors). Empirical 95% confidence bounds derived from 30-day regional residual standard error elevate empirical coverage to \(\ge 90\%\).

The ultimate retail price forecast \(\hat{P}_{r, t+5}\) for any regional market \(r\) is synthesized by composing the mathematical operations from all preceding sections into a unified multi-layer estimator. The architecture transitions sequentially from quantitative futures and qualitative shock memory to regularized regime projection, spatial basis calibration, statutory tax burdens, physical friction offsets, and volatility-gated persistence blending:

Equation 15.1: Master Multi-Layer Retail Price Estimator

$$\begin{aligned} \hat{P}_{r, t+5} &= \lambda_{\text{vol}} \cdot \Big[ \underbrace{\beta_0 + \sum_{k=1}^K \beta_k X_{k, t}}_{\text{Sec 01 \& 11: Quant Futures \& Crack}} + \underbrace{\boldsymbol{\beta}_{\text{shock}}^T \mathbf{M}_t}_{\text{Sec 02 \& 03: Decayed Shock Memory}} + \underbrace{\boldsymbol{\beta}_{\alpha}^T \mathbf{f}_{\text{Qlib}, t}}_{\text{Sec 14: Symbolic Alphas}} \\[8pt] &\qquad\qquad + \underbrace{\Delta_{\text{metro}, r}}_{\text{Sec 06 \& 09: Spatial Basis}} + \underbrace{T_{\text{tax}, r}}_{\text{Sec 08 \& 10: State/CARB Burden}} + \underbrace{\delta_{\text{physical}, r}}_{\text{Sec 07 \& 13: Seismic/River Friction}} \Big] + (1 - \lambda_{\text{vol}}) \cdot P_{r, t} \end{aligned}$$

$$\text{CI}_{95\%}(r, t+5) = \hat{P}_{r, t+5} \pm 1.96 \cdot \sigma_{\text{residual, 30d}}(r)$$
