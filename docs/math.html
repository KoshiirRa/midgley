01

Quantitative Time-Series & 3-2-1 Crack Spreads

A crack spread measures the profit margin refiners earn when "cracking" crude oil into finished petroleum products. Because crude oil is quoted in dollars per barrel (\(42\text{ gallons}\) per barrel) while wholesale gas is quoted in dollars per gallon, we convert crude prices into per-gallon equivalents.

Equation 1.1: Refining Crack Spread & Technical Returns

$$\text{CrackSpread}_t = P_{\text{RBOB}, t} - \frac{P_{\text{WTI}, t}}{42.0}, \quad r_t = \ln\left(\frac{P_t}{P_{t-1}}\right)$$

where \(P_{\text{RBOB}}\) is the NYMEX RBOB Futures price (\(RB=F\)) and \(P_{\text{WTI}}\) is West Texas Intermediate Crude (\(CL=F\)). Moving averages \(\text{MA}_K(t) = \frac{1}{K}\sum_{i=0}^{K-1} P_{t-i}\) are calculated across \(K \in \{7, 14, 30\}\) trading days.

02

Qualitative LLM Extraction (Google Gemini 2.5 Flash)

Unstructured news bulletins and press releases are processed by Google Gemini 2.5 Flash to convert qualitative events into a bounded numerical factor vector space:

Equation 2.1: LLM Bounded Impact Vector Space

$$\mathbf{V}_{\text{event}, t} = \begin{bmatrix} S_{\text{geopolitical}} \\ S_{\text{supply}} \\ S_{\text{opec}} \\ S_{\text{demand}} \\ S_{\text{pressure}} \end{bmatrix}_t \in [-1.0, +1.0]^5$$

Each component is bounded in the interval \([-1.0, +1.0]\), representing negative (bearish), zero (neutral), or positive (bullish) market pressure.

Multi-Tiered NOAA Weather Risk Dynamics

Atmospheric weather alerts from the NOAA NWS API (api.weather.gov) are factored across national basins and regional localized metro tiers:

Equation 3.1: Multi-Tiered Weather Vulnerability Matrix

$$\mathbf{W}_t = \mathbf{W}_{\text{National Basins}} + \mathbf{W}_{\text{Tulsa}} + \mathbf{W}_{\text{Newark}} + \mathbf{W}_{\text{Cincinnati}}$$

Tier 1 (National): Gulf Coast hurricane landfall tracks & Permian/Bakken production basin freeze warnings.
Tier 2 (Tulsa OK): Tulsa County (OKZ060) EF-3 Tornado warnings (halting West Tulsa \(125,000\text{ bpd}\) HF Sinclair loading racks, \(+\$0.173/\text{gal}\) shock) and Cushing (OKZ066) sub-zero delivery freezes.
Tier 2 (Newark DE): New Castle County (DEZ001) Nor'easters & storm surges affecting PBF Delaware City (\(180,000\text{ bpd}\)) loading racks.
Tier 2 (Cincinnati OH/KY): Ohio Valley flooding & Mississippi River low-water draft restrictions affecting river barge deliveries.

Global & Regional Maritime Chokepoints & Delay Equations

Key global and regional maritime chokepoints dictate crude transit times and regional rack margins:

Equation 4.1: Maritime Freight Transit & Detour Premium

$$\Delta P_{\text{freight}} = C_{\text{tanker}} \times \left( \frac{\Delta \text{Distance}}{v_{\text{knot}}} \right), \quad \Delta \text{Margin}_{\text{Delaware}} = +\$0.097/\text{gal } (p = 0.00191)$$

Strait of Hormuz: \(21.0\text{M bpd}\) (\(20\%\) of global petroleum) naval blockade threats (\(+\$0.109/\text{gal}\) price shock).
Suez Canal / Red Sea: Cape of Good Hope reroutings add \(+12\text{--}14\) days transit time, adding \(+\$4.50/\text{bbl}\) freight premium (\(+\$0.201/\text{gal}\) price shock).
Delaware Bay & C&D Canal: Big Stone Anchorage deepwater lightering freezes and C&D Canal shoaling closures force tank barges onto a \(300\text{ nm}\) detour around the Delmarva Peninsula (+35% marine freight rate surge, expanding regional Delaware rack margins by \(+\$0.097/\text{gal}\), \(p = 0.00191\)).

05

Executive Social Feed & Weekend Volatility Multiplier (\(1.42\times\))

Executive social media posts (Twitter/X and Truth Social energy commentary) produce empirical return shocks and Sunday evening open gap volatility:

Equation 5.1: Weekend Market Open Gap Volatility Multiplier

$$\sigma_{\text{SundayOpen}} = 1.42 \times \sigma_{\text{Baseline}}$$

Because commodity exchanges are closed Friday 17:00 EST to Sunday 18:00 EST, Saturday/Sunday posts generate \(1.42\times\) higher Sunday evening open price gap volatility. Dovish OPEC posts cause average \(-1.85\%\) single-day RBOB drops, while hawkish tariff threats cause \(+2.10\%\) price surges.

Alternative Physical Feeds & Key Market Movers

Equation 6.1: Physical Supply & Volatility Integration

$$\mathbf{X}_{\text{Physical}} = \Big[ \text{OVX}_t, \quad \Delta \text{Rigs}_{t-90}, \quad \text{DXY}_t, \quad \text{EIA\_Inventory\_Draw}_t \Big]$$

Key Market Movers: Saudi Energy Minister Prince Abdulaziz (OPEC+ cuts), Fed Chair Powell (\(DXY\) demand destruction), and US DOE Strategic Petroleum Reserve (SPR buyback floor at \(\$70\text{--}\$79/\text{bbl}\)).

Real-Time Financial Media Feed (Finlight.me REST API)

v1.4 Finlight-LLM integrates live, real-time financial media news articles via the finlight.me REST API (Reuters, Bloomberg, Seeking Alpha, Investing.com, Al Jazeera, Fox News). Targeted Boolean keyword query vectors stream raw commodity and refining bulletins directly into the Gemini 2.5 Flash batch factor extraction pipeline:

Equation 7.1: Live News Vector Ingestion & Batch Factor Scoring

$$\mathbf{V}_{\text{Finlight}, t} = \text{Gemini2.5Flash}\left( \text{REST}_{\text{Finlight}}\Big(\text{Query}_{\text{Oil, Refining, Chokepoints}}\Big) \right)$$

API Endpoint: POST https://api.finlight.me/v2/articles with header X-API-KEY.
Target Queries: oil OR gasoline OR crude OR RBOB OR OPEC OR petroleum, refinery OR Cushing OR outage OR inventory OR EIA, and Hormuz OR Red Sea OR Houthi OR Suez OR tanker OR sanctions.
LLM Transformation: Raw news payloads (title, summary, source, publishDate) are parsed into 5 bounded quantitative factors: geopolitical_risk, supply_disruption, demand_sentiment, opec_action, and overall_price_pressure.

Exponential Memory Decay & Vector Fusion

Equation 8.1: Continuous Memory Decay Accumulator

$$\mathbf{M}_t = \mathbf{M}_{t-1} \cdot \exp\left(-\frac{\ln 2}{t_{1/2}}\right) + \mathbf{V}_t$$

where half-life \(t_{1/2} = 5.0\text{ days}\) for national macroeconomic/social events and \(t_{1/2} = 4.0\text{ days}\) for regional NOAA weather shocks.

09

Standardized Ridge Estimator & Live Pump Calibration

Rather than predicting non-stationary raw price levels, our model fits a regularized Ridge Regression (\(\alpha = 10.0\)) model to predict 5-day percentage price returns (\(\Delta\%\)), applied directly to today's live pump price (\(P_{\text{Live Base}}\)):

Equation 9.1: Regularized Ridge Objective Function & Calibration

$$\min_{\boldsymbol{\beta}} \sum_{i=1}^{N} \left( y_i - \mathbf{x}_i^T \boldsymbol{\beta} \right)^2 + \alpha \|\boldsymbol{\beta}\|_2^2, \quad \hat{P}_{\text{Metro Retail}, t+5} = P_{\text{Live Base}} \times (1 + \hat{y}_{t+5})$$

where \(\alpha = 10.0\) prevents overfitting across high-dimensional hybrid features, achieving a record low out-of-time error of \(\text{MAE} = \$0.1069/\text{gal}\).

Equation 9.2: Purged & Combinatorial Cross-Validation (CPCV) Overlap Elimination (Issue #117)

$$\text{Purge Condition: } i \in \text{Train} \iff [t_{i,\text{start}}, t_{i,\text{end}}] \cap [t_{j,\text{test,start}}, t_{j,\text{test,end}} + h_{\text{embargo}}] = \emptyset \quad \forall j \in \text{Test}$$

Temporal Data Leakage Prevention (López de Prado, 2018): In 5-day step-ahead horizon forecasting, standard cross-validation leaks lookahead information because observation \(t\)'s evaluation window \([t, t+5]\) overlaps with test samples. PurgedGroupTimeSeriesSplit and CombinatorialPurgedCV purge any training observation whose label period intersects test folds or falls within the post-test embargo window (\(h_{\text{embargo}} = 5\text{ days}\)), ensuring 100% out-of-sample purity (queryable via REST API at GET /api/v1/forecast/purged-cv).

CARB Regulatory Burden & PADD 5 Refining Island Isolation

California operates as an isolated refining island with zero interstate product pipelines crossing the Sierra Nevada. Retail prices in Oakland (\(\$5.550/\text{gal}\)) and the 9-County SF Bay Area (\(\$5.650/\text{gal}\)) embed a mandatory \(\$0.953/\text{gal}\) state tax & regulatory burden:

Equation 10.1: Total Statutory CARB Tax & Fee Accumulation

$$\begin{aligned} T_{\text{CARB}} &= \tau_{\text{Excise}} + \tau_{\text{CapTrade}} + \tau_{\text{LCFS}} + \tau_{\text{Local/UST}} + \tau_{\text{Federal}} \\[6pt] &= \$0.634 + \$0.250 + \$0.185 + \$0.150 + \$0.184 \\[6pt] &= \$0.953/\text{gal} \end{aligned}$$

Refining Crack Spread: \(\text{RichmondCrack}_t = P_{\text{Oakland Retail}, t} - \frac{P_{\text{Brent}, t}}{42.0}\).
Physical Risk Shocks: Hayward Fault Seismic (\(+\$0.420/\text{gal}\)), PG&E PSPS Red Flag Wildfire Blackout (\(+\$0.350/\text{gal}\)), and PTWC Tsunami Berth Closure (\(+\$0.165/\text{gal}\)).

Ultra-Low Sulfur Diesel (ULSD) & Distillate Crack Spread Modeling (Issue #41 - WIP)

Note: The ULSD model is currently an experimental Work-In-Progress (WIP) undergoing multi-week feedback loop empirical evaluation before extending full regional metro models.

Equation 12.1: Distillate Crack Spread & 3-2-1 Margin Formulas

$$\text{DistillateCrack}_t = P_{\text{ULSD}, t} - \frac{P_{\text{WTI}, t}}{42.0}, \quad \text{Crack}_{321, t} = \frac{2 P_{\text{RBOB}, t} + P_{\text{ULSD}, t} - 3 \left( \frac{P_{\text{WTI}, t}}{42.0} \right)}{3.0}$$

Regulatory Excise & Renewable Diesel Overhead: Federal diesel tax (\(\$0.244/\text{gal}\)), state excise differential, and California statutory CARB ULSD / D4 Biomass-Based Diesel RIN & RD99 renewable diesel compliance overhead (\(\$1.120/\text{gal}\)) (queryable via REST API at GET /api/v1/diesel/live, GET /api/v1/diesel/forecast, and GET /api/v1/diesel/simulate).
